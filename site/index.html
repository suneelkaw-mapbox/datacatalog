Mapbox Boundaries

Global boundary datasets for analytics, mapping, and data enrichment

Read the docs →Contact sales
How it ships

Licensing
Contact sales for a contract; a token alone does not unlock this dataset.
APIs
License gives you access to tilesets or GeoJSON files with raw geometry and metadata
Format
Vector tiles (MVT); GeoJSONL files
5 levels inside
  • AdministrativeDivisions within a country or territory delineated for general administration, usually with a capital and a government that can set laws and collect taxes. Five levels from countries (adm0) to fourth-level areas (adm4), plus boundary lines and label points.Docs
  • PostalApproximate mail-delivery areas built from national postal code systems. Three grouping levels (pos1 to pos3) above the full-detail postal code (pos4).Docs
  • StatisticalNon-administrative boundaries used mainly for statistics: areas defined by a statistical bureau, or historical boundaries still used for reporting (for example US census units). Five levels from the coarsest reporting region (sta1) to the finest (sta5).Docs
  • LocalityPlaces people recognize as a 'place' even when they are not official boundaries: metropolitan areas, cities and towns, districts and neighborhoods.Docs
  • LegislativeConstituency areas for legislative assemblies and councils, where voters typically elect one representative per area. Five levels from national upper houses (leg1) to local councils (leg5).Docs
